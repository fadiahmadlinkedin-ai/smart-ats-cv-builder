# Action verbs and keywords

Word banks for writing bullets and skills. Use them as prompts, not as filler. Every word must describe something the candidate really did or uses. Industry keywords count only when they match the candidate's real experience and the target job advert. The advert's own wording always beats this list.

## 1. English action verbs

Start each bullet with a different verb in the past tense (present tense for the current role is also fine if used consistently). Avoid "Responsible for", "Helped", "Worked on", "Assisted in".

## 3. Industry keyword banks
